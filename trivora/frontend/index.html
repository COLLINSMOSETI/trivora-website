How we create websites

A clear process from idea to launch

You always know what is happening and what comes next. Here is how every Trivora project moves from a conversation to a live website.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We listen first. We learn about your business, your customers and your goals, then agree on the pages, features and budget before any work begins.

  2. 02

    Design

    We design the look and feel of your site — layout, colours, typography and user journey — and show it to you for feedback and approval.

  3. 03

    Build

    Our developers turn the approved design into clean, fast code that works on every screen size, with search-engine-friendly structure built in.

  4. 04

    Integrate

    We connect the backend, database, contact forms, payments (such as M-Pesa), email and any other systems your business depends on.

  5. 05

    Test & launch

    We test on phones, tablets and desktops, check speed and security, set up your domain and hosting, and take your site live.

  6. 06

    Support & grow

    After launch we stay with you — fixing issues, making updates and adding new features as your business grows.

Systems integration

Make your tools work as one system

Most businesses run on a mix of disconnected tools. We link them together so information moves automatically, errors drop and your team saves hours every week.

  • Payments — M-Pesa (Daraja), cards and online checkout connected to your site or app.
  • Customer data — website forms feeding straight into your CRM, database or spreadsheet.
  • Operations — inventory, point-of-sale, accounting and order systems that stay in sync.
  • Messaging — automatic SMS, email and WhatsApp notifications for customers and staff.
  • Custom APIs — we build and connect APIs so any two systems can talk to each other.
  • Data migration — safely moving your records from old systems to new ones.

FAQ

Questions we often hear

What do I need to get started?

Just an idea of what your business does and what you want the website to achieve. If you have a logo, photos or content, great — if not, we will guide you on what is needed.

How much does a website cost?

Website development starts from KSh 30,000. The final price depends on the number of pages, features and integrations. Tell us what you need and we will give you a clear quote.

Can you connect my website to M-Pesa?

Yes. We integrate mobile payments and other systems so customers can pay online and your records update automatically.

Can you work with the systems I already use?

In most cases, yes. We connect existing tools through their APIs or build a bridge between them. Contact us with the systems you use and we will confirm what is possible.

Do you help with hosting and domain names?

Yes. We can help you choose and set up a domain and hosting, and keep everything running after launch.

Will I be able to see who contacts my business?

Yes. Our websites can include a backend with a database and an admin dashboard so you can see every enquiry and manage it in one place.
